03 / KNOW THE LIMITS

Inspectable by design.

Data contract

One row per calendar day, store and SKU. Include zero-sales days. Inventory is end-of-day stock; discount is the total discount on gross units. Returns reverse discounted revenue and recover product cost, assuming resalable goods.

date, store, sku, category, units, unit_price, unit_cost, discount, returns, inventory, lead_days

The currency selector changes display labels only. Supply one currency per file; no foreign exchange conversion is performed.

Validation & model evidence

Backtests predict each of the last 28 observations using only earlier observations, with at least 28 training days. WAPE is total absolute error divided by total units; zero denominators are shown as unavailable.

Planning uses the latest stock snapshot for each SKU and estimates coverage over lead time plus buffer days. Missing daily records are reported; forecasts with gaps require caution.

This release is a browser analytics prototype. No deployed MLOps, causal impact, savings claims or enterprise service guarantees. The repository includes automated analytical checks and a Pages deployment workflow.
